Frequently Asked Questions

How does the farmer subscription work?

Our farmer subscription provides Sindhudurg/Konkan farmers with access to organic farming resources, market connections, and certification support to help you grow and sell your organic produce effectively.

Can hotels customize their orders?

Yes, our hotel premium plan includes customized order planning based on your menu requirements and seasonal availability of organic produce.
